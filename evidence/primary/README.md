# Primary Empirical Research

This directory contains evidence briefs for original empirical studies.

## Evidence briefs

- [Copilot and a standardized programming task](2023-copilot-task-productivity.md) — Peng et al., arXiv v1 (2023); randomized positive time result conditional on task completion, with assignment and completer denominators separated.
- [METR: familiar open-source tasks with early-2025 tools](2025-metr-experienced-developer-productivity.md) — July 2025 paper v2 and author report; randomized increase in active task time in a bounded setting.
- [METR: follow-up selection and measurement limits](2026-metr-productivity-design-update.md) — February 2026 original research update; later experiment whose present-day magnitude the authors consider unreliable.
- [Writing Code vs. Shipping Code: Productivity Effects Across Generations of AI Coding Tools](2026-writing-code-vs-shipping-code.md) — NBER Working Paper 35275 (May 2026); large-scale matched event study across successive generations of AI coding tools.

## Inclusion criteria

Eligible materials include:

- peer-reviewed research;
- working papers with disclosed methods;
- randomized or controlled experiments;
- large-scale observational studies;
- original measurement reports with inspectable methodology.

A source's inclusion here does not imply that every claim in the source is correct or that its results generalize universally. Each brief must state publication status, research design, scope, measured findings, model-derived findings, and limitations.

Do not place the repository's own conclusions in this directory unless they are clearly labeled as **repository interpretation**.
