## Method and reported observations

More than 40 internal developers in the US and Asia performed code-generation, refactoring and documentation tasks over several weeks with assisted and unassisted work. Timing, demonstrations, automated code checks and feedback are distinct measures. This small internal task study is not a representative enterprise trial.

Local time gains coexist with small savings on complex tasks and slower work for some less-experienced participants. The article recommends training, coaching, risk controls, baselining and reallocating capacity. Favorable completion and code-check observations remain visible. Advice is not an observed buyer action.

## Limits, interests and dependence
