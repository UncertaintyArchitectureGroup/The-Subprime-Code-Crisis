## Interpretation and limits

**Authors:** ambitious agendas, workflow redesign, validation and other management
practices correlate with high performance; their relative-weights analysis uses 31
predictors (p 15). Commercial consulting affiliations and recommendations matter
for interpretation; they do not establish misconduct.

**Repository:** adoption, reported benefits and delivery economics are different
outcomes. This supports balanced enterprise context in R1 Ch 3, not a causal
execution-mandate explanation, individual productivity estimate, universal
transformation budget or proof of vendor intent. Self-report, selection,
weighting and attribution limits remain; no uncertainty interval or raw-data
causal ROI reproduction is claimed. This original is distinct from the retired
Banandre derivative S-2025-02.
