# Stack Overflow: reported AI attitudes in 2024

**P-2024-01.** Stack Overflow, *2024 Developer Survey*,
[AI results](https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2024/ai) and
[methodology](https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2024/methodology).
Fixed annual edition 2024; no exact publication day is asserted from these pages.
Original online questionnaire, not a productivity experiment or peer-reviewed
causal study. Both complete pages read 20 Oct 2026 UTC / 21 Oct Europe/Warsaw.

## Design and question-level findings

Methods:65,437 qualified responses from 185 countries; fielded 19 May–20 June 2024;
recruitment mainly through Stack Overflow's own channels. Engaged/self-selected
users are overrepresented; questions have different response subsets.

AI§3.3 asks **“Do you believe AI is a threat to your current job?”** The
professional-developer tab has **28,142** responses: **69.6% No**, 18.7% unsure,
11.7% Yes (sum 100%). The publisher's prose rounds No to 70%. The all-respondent
tab is a different denominator, 36,328, with 68.3% No. Neither subgroup is the
whole 65,437 survey sample.

Positive attitudes remain visible: AI§3.1 favorability has 45,873 answers;
23.6% very favorable plus 48.3% favorable =71.9% (publisher rounds 72%). Reported
use, anticipated benefits and confidence questions do not measure task time or
realized productivity. The page also reports doubts about accuracy and complex
tasks. No overtime or causal fear-to-output measure is present in these reviewed
questions. No longitudinal respondent panel or rising job-fear estimate is
established by this annual result.

## Interpretation and placement

**Publisher:** largely favorable attitudes coexist with trust/context concerns.
**Repository:** this limits a general fear-driven adoption story; it does not show
that no developer is anxious. Keep the short balanced context beside the
work-intensity hypothesis in R1 Ch 1, outside the experimental-results table.

No raw-data reanalysis, population inference, causal estimate or CI is claimed.
Stack Overflow has commercial AI/community products; disclose that context
without alleging distortion. The survey dataset is not ingested, redistributed
or independently analyzed, so no separate DS record is created.
