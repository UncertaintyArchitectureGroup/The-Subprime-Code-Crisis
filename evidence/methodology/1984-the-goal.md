## Citation and access boundary

Eliyahu M. Goldratt and Jeff Cox, *The Goal: A Process of Ongoing Improvement*, North River Press, first published 1984. The [publisher's anniversary notice](https://northriverpress.com/30th-anniversary-edition-of-the-goal-published/), inspected 21 October 2026, confirms authorship, manufacturing origin and later edition changes. This notice is bibliographic evidence, **not the original book**. No complete identified book edition was obtained or read for this audit. Edition/page-level claims, detailed original concepts and a complete methodology review remain unresolved.

## Proposed application and assumptions

The report uses constraint, inventory and local/system improvement as a lens. Its software queue mechanism is explicitly **repository interpretation**, conditional on admitted work, task/risk mix, service demand, effective capacity, variation and work/release policies. It is not a theorem quoted from the unread 1984 edition or an empirical AI effect. Coding may be the initial constraint, a non-constraint, or one of several changing constraints; faster coding may improve delivery.

## Limits and competing explanations

This record supports no measured AI-review multiplier, fixed human capacity, inevitable harm or universal single bottleneck. Demand, discovery, task selection, release batching, coordination and automation can change outcomes. Full original review and comparison with other flow/queue frameworks remain open. The [claim graph](../../report/appendices/mechanism-hypotheses.md) states proposed assumptions and falsifiers without assigning them to Goldratt as observed findings.
