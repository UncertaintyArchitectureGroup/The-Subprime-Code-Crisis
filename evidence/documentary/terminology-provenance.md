## Bounded finding

The inspected public record shows multiple uses of related terminology across 2025 and 2026. The earliest located record in this review is Nick Vigier's use of **Subprime Code** on 2025-09-16. The earliest located record using the full phrase **The Subprime Code Crisis** is Igor Światowski's dated article listing on 2025-12-16. Richard Ewing's located article is dated 2026-05-01.

These records support the conclusion that the terminology was in public circulation before or alongside the development of this repository. They do **not** establish a single originator, a complete lineage, or that one framework derives from another.

## Repository interpretation

The most defensible attribution model is **independent conceptual convergence**: multiple practitioners examining similar AI-assisted-development conditions adopted related mortgage-crisis language. This repository therefore does not claim coinage of the phrase.

The repository's attributable contribution is the evidence-governed delivery-system synthesis, its separation of empirical findings from system inference and warning scenarios, its mechanism of bottleneck migration and deferred verification cost, and its operational protocol set.
