## Bounded finding

The inspected public record shows multiple uses of related terminology across 2025 and 2026. Nick Vigier publicly used **Subprime Code** and “subprime coding crisis” on 2025-09-16. A currently accessible Richard Ewing/CareerWin surface displays the full phrase with a 2025-11-12 date. Igor Światowski's public profile lists another full-phrase article dated 2025-12-16.

Richard Ewing's current canonical page claims an earlier April 2025 origin, but the present review did not independently verify the claimed Built In/HackerNoon origin publication. His current publications catalog separately dates the Beehiiv work to March 2026. The repository therefore records the April 2025 date as an **unverified provenance claim**, not as established chronology.

These records support the conclusion that the terminology was in public circulation across multiple independent works. They do **not** establish a single originator, a complete lineage, ownership of the phrase, copying, or derivation among the respective frameworks.

## Repository interpretation

The most defensible attribution model remains **independent conceptual convergence** unless evidence of influence or derivation is located. The repository therefore does not claim coinage of the phrase.

The repository's attributable contribution is the evidence-governed delivery-system synthesis, its separation of empirical findings from system inference and warning scenarios, its mechanism of bottleneck migration and deferred verification cost, and its operational protocol set.
