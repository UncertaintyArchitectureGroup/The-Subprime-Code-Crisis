# Article drafting scope

This file supplements [root AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md); it cannot override its evidence flows, approval or independent-review gates. The root's Article and publishing boundaries section owns this scoped protocol.

Use a complete editorial blueprint and a separate publication-facing manuscript. The blueprint owns the argument plan, provenance, claim types, counterexamples, source needs, exclusions and unresolved decisions. The manuscript owns continuous reader-facing prose. Do not paste agent instructions or editorial checklists into the article.

For substantial revisions, read both documents in full, select a coherent section block, revise it in context, reread the whole manuscript, and reconcile the blueprint. Keep material counterarguments and disconfirming observations visible. Do not introduce a new canonical term when the existing glossary already owns the concern.

An AI dialogue can prompt a hypothesis; it cannot validate that hypothesis or supply verified statistics. Separate the maintainer's contribution from model elaboration. New empirical reliance must follow the root source flow, including registry/current-use synchronization where applicable. A research target listed in a blueprint is not a supporting citation.

Distribution status belongs to tools/publishing/publications.json. A draft label does not excuse unsupported factual claims, and a published label does not establish source verification. Changes to report conclusions or protocols require their own owning-artifact review; a new article must not silently rewrite them.
