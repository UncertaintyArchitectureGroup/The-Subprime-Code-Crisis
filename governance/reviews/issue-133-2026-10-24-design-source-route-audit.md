## Competing explanations and symmetric tests

- **DES-1 adverse claim (Open):** Cheaper implementation might expand coupling and dependencies faster than design assessment/competent ownership adapt, harming later modification/maintenance. Competing explanations are purposeful new functionality, architectural simplification, stronger existing boundaries, working automated design checks, task mix, inherited architecture and confounding selection. **Weaken harm:** stable/improved later safe-change effort, explanation/change/diagnosis/recovery capability and actual whole-system value at acceptable total effort when development accelerates. A larger static metric alone cannot decide.
- **DES-2 remedy claim (Open):** Suitable boundaries, explicit invariants, appropriate architecture checks, competent owners and testable substitutes may improve later change/recovery cost while preserving AI gains. **Weaken remedy:** no worthwhile incremental outcome or higher total burden compared with unchanged real Design practice; improvement because of other factors/learning not evidence this intervention caused it. No architecture/documentation quota or mandated approval sequence follows.
