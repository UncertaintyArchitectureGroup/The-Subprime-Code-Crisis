- Baseline U denotes unassessed actual delivery, not universal absence of a function.
  Source-specific observed task outcomes remain useful bounded findings.
- Counts/time/proxies, actual competence, user outcomes and lifecycle return are
  different endpoints. The card table proposes observables; it reports no invented
  local baseline, treatment effect, confidence interval or empirical pilot.
- Candidate remedies and receiving owners are proposed research designs, not new
  mandatory production gates or actual consent to tradeoffs. Local studies must
  fix meaningful margins/windows before evaluating results; no claim of retrospective
  preregistration or universal threshold.
- Positive task/release gains, mixed/uncertain findings, a scoring counterexample
  and successful unchanged/adapted practice remain visible. Cost increases can be
  justified by benefits; critical losses cannot disappear into an aggregate score.
- One macro and one micro source do not automatically establish a particular causal
  link. No full-chain triangulated verdict, burnout prevalence, ownership loss,
