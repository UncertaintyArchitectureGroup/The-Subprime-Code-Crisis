## What was repaired

executed queries, 149 displayed hits and 108 distinct returned URLs**. Each query
has its exact string and an ordered hit list. The catalog records title/URL,
selection or rejection reason, duplicate identity and candidate/source link.
The order is the tool's displayed order, not a claim about the search engine's
complete ranking. Three restricted calls returned no objects; that says nothing
about whether matching literature exists.

No query selected more than five relevant original leads. Metadata-only and
contextual leads do not acquire an empirical verdict. The original strategy,
original batch log and D01–D23 records are unchanged. They remain dated history;
their missing per-query order has not retrospectively become known.

The [same Candidate Register's continuation](issue-111-2026-10-23-candidates-continuation.json)
adds **D24–D51: 28 research identities**. It is not another accepted-source registry.
Repeated papers, known METR/Xu source anchors, media retellings and encyclopedias
are distinguished. All title-level exclusions remain in the return ledger.
The first pass's six citation/version follow-ups remain in the original log;
this correction adds no new topical query family or such follow-up search.

Before opening additional originals, six were selected for direct question fit,
comparative design and inspectability. The total is **18 previous + 6 additional
unique full-text/passage screens = 24**, the frozen cap. Other new objects remain
metadata-only and are held or used only as context. No effect-sign requirement
was used for selection.
