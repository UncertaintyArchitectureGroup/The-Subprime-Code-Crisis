## Human decision and adequacy analysis

The maintainer's 2026-10-22 instruction, recorded in #85, explicitly authorizes
updating AGENTS and workflows, creating the necessary issues and fixing the plan.
It requires every node to list what it gave the team in the old process; Review
must be both a quality gate and an ownership/system-understanding gate. Evaluate
AI improvement across all node outcomes. Complete one issue through merge, then
reread/revise the plan, show the next task and wait for approval before starting it.

The current rules already separate evidence review, integration, approval and
independent review. They lack the explicit complete-outcome assessment and the
post-merge next-issue checkpoint. This change extends the existing procedures,
not a new source flow or automated approval mechanism. The user's current request
authorizes #85's implementation, correction, review and merge; other tasks remain
queued. Materially different future treatment still needs its own decision.

Alternatives considered: preserve generic existing rules; assess only speed and
quality; retain every manual ceremony; execute the entire roadmap without further
checkpoints. None implements the requested direction. The selected method allows
functional substitutes and disclosed tradeoffs, compares actual imperfect baselines,
and treats unknowns as unknowns. Its evaluation obligations do not imply that
every historical team successfully received every intended benefit.

The original #84 plan remains intact beneath a current-plan pointer. Its dated
amendment clarifies that one rigorous study can support its own bounded proposition;
macro/micro triangulation is useful when independent and scope-compatible, not a
quota. No existing research verdict or source status changes with method adoption.
