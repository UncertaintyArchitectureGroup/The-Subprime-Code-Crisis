These leads do not select a universal winner. A delivery gain can coexist with
more review burden. A learning intervention can improve immediate completion
without establishing delayed capability; conversely, an uncertain delayed
contrast cannot be called demonstrated harm. A narrow control benefit does not
establish full lifecycle return. These are provisional comparison boundaries,
not changed H0, HUM, REV or ECO verdicts.

### Three reading traps caught during screening

- **D09:** the absolute retest comparison and the decline from initial performance
  are different endpoints. The corrected tests do not justify a simple claim of
  a statistically established delayed deficit.
- **D20:** moving from 25% to 60% completion is a **35-percentage-point** difference,
  not a 35% relative improvement. The participants and effort boundary matter.
- **D23:** the public 25% PR headline concerns respondent estimates of low-quality
  **and/or** AI-generated submissions. It is not an audited fraction of all PRs;
  the report also contains favorable time estimates, with different item samples.

The exact source locators and uncertainties are recorded under those candidate
IDs. The two educational learning papers, D09/D21, and the observational
enterprise cases must not be spliced into one observed causal chain.

Nonresearch navigation/news results are categorized, not a complete hit census.

four-query correction allowance is not authorization for an unbounded search.

Unknown funding or conflict information stays unknown, not “none.”

Failure to locate an endpoint in this packet is not evidence that no such study
exists. This is an exploratory bounded search, not an exhaustive systematic review.
