## Questions

### Q1 — Capacity, adaptation and task mix
H0-A/B; COD, REV, INTG; ALT-1/2/3/5. Population: professional and OSS software
developers/teams. Student/laboratory tasks are adjacent, not enterprise evidence.
Compare AI-assisted versus concurrent controls, pre/post trajectories or assistance
modes with comparable work. Seek active review/integration effort, queue/waiting,
accepted useful output, quality and capability. Separate same-session, <=4-week
and >4-week observations. Record tool/staff/process changes and selection.
An observed rise in admitted demand with sustained safe outcomes could favor
adequate capability or adaptation. Persistent waiting or burden at comparable
scope could favor mismatch. Artifact counts alone cannot decide between them.

### Q2 — Understanding retained over time
HUM-1; COD/REV/OPS; ALT-1/2/5/6b. Compare assistance modes, explanation/practice
substitutes and unaided work. Seek unaided explanation, diagnosis, modification,
recovery and transfer to non-authors. Separate immediate, >=7-day delayed and
shared-team outcomes. Effective substitutes and contrary/null results matter;
self-reported confidence and task completion are not retention measurements.

### Q3 — Full costs and a control's added value
ECO; H0-D/R; BAL/VAL; ALT-6a/6b. Separate adoption versus previous/no-AI workflow
from a named additional control versus the same AI workflow without it. Seek
comparable useful outputs/service, all-role effort, review/rework, later maintenance
and critical quality/capability outcomes. Separate session cost from >=4-week or
lifecycle follow-up. A positive adoption effect is not a tested control effect;
more review effort alone is not negative net value.

## Windows, channels and stopping rule

Publications 2022-01-01 through 2026-10-23. Earlier foundational work can be
context-only. English search; relevant Ukrainian/Polish/Russian material may be
screened without claiming comprehensive multilingual coverage. Public data only.

Use general web indexing, scholarly/primary-domain queries, original author/lab/
institution/vendor pages and backward references. Attempt native scholarly search
where accessible. A domain-restricted web query is NOT a native database search.
Access failures are disclosed, never converted into completed channel coverage.

Nine families below, each with one general and one scholarly/primary query:
**18 planned queries**. Inspect up to the first five relevant unique original
research-object leads per query, retaining duplicates and dispositions. At most
six citation/version lookups plus four omission-correction queries during review.
Full-text screening cap: 24 unique new research objects; overflow stays Hold,
prioritized by question fit/comparative design/inspectability, never effect sign.
Stop on completed fixed coverage and review corrections, not a favorable result.
This is bounded discovery, not an exhaustive systematic review.

## Inclusion and exclusion

Include original empirical studies or transparent first-party technical reports
with identifiable authors/date/version and inspectable scope/method or enough
abstract detail for provisional screening. Preprint/vendor origin is not exclusion.
Use author, publisher or authoritative repository versions, not media retellings.
Record tool/data ownership, funding/conflicts and independence; unknown stays unknown.

Hold methods/identity/access-inadequate candidates, including potentially important
abstract-only objects. Exclude duplicate retellings, promotional assertions without
inspectable methods, out-of-window items and unrelated outcomes. Nonsoftware learning
can be context-only, not direct evidence. Accepted unchanged core studies are context
anchors, not newly discovered independent confirmations. Preserve all screened
objects, including excluded/held/duplicate objects, with explicit reasons.

## Candidate fields and independent review

For each candidate record ID, citation/canonical URL/version/publication status,
query provenance/date, relevance and hypothesis IDs, provisional result/direction/
uncertainty and distinguishing observation, inclusion/disposition reason, entity,
funding, conflicts, design/population/tasks/tool/comparator/outcomes/horizon,
external validity, dependence/replication, access depth, and exactly one route:
Accept for Flow A / Route to Flow C / Hold / Context only / Reject.
No paper-level final assessment is implied by discovery screening.

A separate reviewer must rerun representative queries through multiple accessible
channels, look for omitted favorable/adverse/null/replication/critique evidence,
inspect excluded/held samples, challenge identities/version/conflicts/routing and
coverage, and report actual access limits. Green tests or code-only review are not
Independent Search Review. Missing checks remain pending.
